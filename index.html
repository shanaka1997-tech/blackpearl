Frequently Asked Questions

What are your shipping rates? +
Our rates depend on the package weight, destination, and delivery speed. Standard delivery starts at $9.99, Express at $24.99, and Premium Express at $49.99. Contact us for custom quotes on bulk shipments.
How do I track my package? +
All packages include tracking. Once your shipment is dispatched, you'll receive a tracking number via email. You can use this number on our website to get real-time updates on your package location.
Do you offer international shipping? +
Yes! We offer international shipping through our Express Delivery and Premium Express packages. We ship to over 150 countries worldwide. International delivery times vary by destination.
What's your return and refund policy? +
We offer a 100% refund if your package doesn't arrive within the promised timeframe. You can request a refund within 30 days of purchase. Our team will investigate and process your refund promptly.
Can I insure my package? +
Yes, we offer optional package insurance for an additional fee. This covers loss, damage, and theft. Insurance can be added during checkout when you book your shipment.
